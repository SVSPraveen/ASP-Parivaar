About Us

A nationwide people’s movement rooted in selfless service.

Welcome To Our Organisation

AJEEVAN SAMARPAN PARIVAAR

Founded by social entrepreneur Atul Kumar Mishra, Ajeevan Samarpan Parivaar works to uplift society through impactful programs in education, healthcare, environment, youth development, and women empowerment.

We believe real change begins at the grassroots level. Our approach blends awareness, action, and leadership to inspire every citizen to serve Bharat with pride and purpose.

"To serve Bharat, beyond self."

👁️ Vision

To ignite the soul of Bharat with awareness, unity, and unstoppable energy. We envision a future where every child is educated, every citizen is conscious, and our nation stands tall as a global leader in humanity.

🎯 Mission

Through Vision Bharat 2026, our mission is to empower the next generation. We conduct mega events, grassroots workshops, school programs, and public campaigns to awaken every heart—to rise, act, and transform.

🚀 Goal

We are committed to gathering 10,000 voices and touching over 1 crore lives across India. This is not just a target; it is a promise to our motherland. This is our Bharat in action.
