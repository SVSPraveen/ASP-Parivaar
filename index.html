Our Mission

Ajeevan Samarpan Parivaar is not just an NGO; it is a nationwide movement. We believe that real change begins at the grassroots level. From classrooms to villages, we are dedicated to empowering the youth, supporting the underprivileged, and keeping our environment clean.

Core Initiatives

📚

Education for All

Providing free study materials, tuition, and mentorship to bridge the learning gap for underprivileged children. Every child deserves a future.

❤️

Healthcare & Yoga

Organizing health camps and community Yoga sessions to promote holistic physical and mental well-being for all citizens.

🌱

Clean & Green India

Leading massive tree plantation drives and cleanliness campaigns to protect Mother Earth for future generations.
